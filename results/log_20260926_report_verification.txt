=== K. EGARCH recursion as implemented by arch ===
\ln\sigma_{t}^{2}=\omega
    +\sum_{i=1}^{p}\alpha_{i}
    \left(\left|e_{t-i}\right|-\sqrt{2/\pi}\right)
    +\sum_{j=1}^{o}\gamma_{j} e_{t-j}
    +\sum_{k=1}^{q}\beta_{k}\ln\sigma_{t-k}^{2}

where :math:`e_{t}=\epsilon_{t}/\sigma_{t}`.
